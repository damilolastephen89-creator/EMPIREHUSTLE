# 🏰 EmpireHustle Logo Concept

## 🎯 Purpose
The logo is the **visual crown** of the EmpireHustle Network. It should embody discipline, growth, and strength while remaining modern and versatile across GitHub, Strikingly, and dashboards.

## 🧩 Core Elements
- **Symbol**:  
  - A stylized **crown** or **empire crest** to represent authority and vision.  
  - Integrated **chart/arrow motif** to symbolize growth and trading success.  
  - Optional **network nodes** to reflect technology and scalability.  

- **Typography**:  
  - Bold, modern sans-serif font (e.g., *Montserrat Bold*).  
  - Wordmark: **EmpireHustle** with emphasis on “Empire” (authority) and “Hustle” (drive).  

- **Color Palette** (from Branding Guide):  
  - **Empire Gold (#FFD700)** → highlights, crown accents.  
  - **Midnight Black (#000000)** → background, text.  
  - **Emerald Green (#00A86B)** → growth arrows, ROI visuals.  

## 🎨 Variations
- **Primary Logo**: Crown + wordmark (gold on black).  
- **Secondary Logo**: Simplified crown icon (for dashboards, favicon).  
- **Monochrome Version**: Black/white for print or minimal contexts.  

## 📂 Usage
- **GitHub** → Place in `/assets/branding/logo.png` and `/assets/branding/logo-dark.png`.  
- **Strikingly** → Use primary logo on homepage and About page.  
- **Dashboards** → Use simplified crown icon for watermarking charts.
