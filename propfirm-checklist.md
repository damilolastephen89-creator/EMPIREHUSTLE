# 🏦 Prop Firm Readiness Checklist

## 🎯 Purpose
This checklist prepares the **EmpireHustle Network** for prop firm evaluations, ensuring performance, risk management, and documentation meet professional standards.

## 📈 Performance & Track Record
- Minimum trade history: 200 trades or 6 months of live-equivalent simulation  
- Win rate: ≥ 65% on top strategies  
- Sharpe ratio: ≥ 1.0  
- Max drawdown: ≤ 10%  
- Consistency: No more than 2 consecutive losing weeks
- 
## 🧱 Strategy Robustness
- Strategy inventory with version numbers and concise rules  
- Edge proof: explanation of why each strategy works  
- Stress tests: high-volatility and low-liquidity scenarios documented  
- Parameter stability: performance across reasonable ranges  

## ⚖️ Risk Management & Execution
- Position-sizing rules (fixed fractional or volatility-based)  
- Daily and intraday loss limits  
- Order execution logs showing fills and slippage  
- Stop-loss and take-profit rules consistently applied  
- Leverage plan documented per strategy  

## 📂 Operational Readiness
- Live-equivalent trade journal with screenshots and timestamps  
- Performance dashboard exports (ROI, win rates, confidence scores)  
- Confidence scoring method explained with examples  
- Versioned playbook showing strategy evolution  
- Contingency runbook for outages and emergencies  

## 📑 Compliance & Application Materials
- Consolidated trade records and broker statements  
- Clear labeling of simulated vs. live results  
- Risk statement: max drawdown, allowed instruments, prohibited behavior  
- Application packet: one-page summary, representative trade replays, dashboard links  

## 🚀 Final Tests & Go/No-Go Gates
- Paper-to-live transition test (1 month with full logging)  
- Consistency gate: monthly return volatility within expected bands  
- Audit readiness: all files accessible and time-stamped  
- Go decision: meet thresholds, pass stress tests, complete runbook
