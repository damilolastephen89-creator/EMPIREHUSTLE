# 📘 EmpireHustle Playbook

## 🎯 Purpose
The Playbook is the living document of the **EmpireHustle Network**, capturing strategy rules, upgrades, and lessons learned. It ensures discipline, consistency, and continuous refinement.

## 🧱 Core Strategies

### 1. MA Crossover (v1.3)
- **Rules**:  
  - Enter long when short-term MA crosses above long-term MA with RSI > 50.  
  - Enter short when short-term MA crosses below long-term MA with RSI < 50.  
- **Stop-Loss**: 1.5% below/above entry.  
- **Take-Profit**: 2:1 reward-to-risk ratio.  
- **Upgrade Notes**: Added RSI divergence filter for precision.

### 2. Breakout (v1.2)
- **Rules**:  
  - Enter on breakout above resistance with volume confirmation.  
  - Retest rule: wait for price to retest breakout zone before entry.  
- **Stop-Loss**: Below breakout zone.  
- **Take-Profit**: Trail stops after 2R.  
- **Upgrade Notes**: Added volume retest rule to reduce false signals.

### 3. Scalping (v1.1)
- **Rules**:  
  - Trade high-liquidity pairs during peak sessions.  
  - Enter on micro support/resistance breaks.  
- **Stop-Loss**: 0.5% max.  
- **Take-Profit**: 1:1.5 reward-to-risk ratio.  
- **Upgrade Notes**: Tightened SL to improve R:R ratio.

## 🧠 Lessons Learned
- **Discipline beats intuition**: Trade filters prevent emotional entries.  
- **Confidence scoring works**: High-confidence setups consistently outperform.  
- **Evolution is key**: Strategies must adapt to market conditions.  
- **Documentation matters**: Replay logs and archives accelerate learning.
